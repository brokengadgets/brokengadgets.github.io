Broken Gadgets R&D

We build things, break things, and build things that break things.

Broken Gadgets R&D is an independent hardware, firmware, and infrastructure engineering lab focused on custom electronics, embedded control systems, self-hosted security tooling, and reverse engineering.

Focus Areas & Open-Source Work

  • Infrastructure & Access Control — Lightweight edge authentication and network plumbing, including haproxy-github-oauth for gating VPN-backed internal services behind HAProxy Lua JWT verification and GitHub Team ACLs.
  • Embedded Hardware & Instrumentation — Custom KiCad PCBs, microcontroller firmware, and lab control interfaces such as OpenMS-controller (WIP).
  • Build Logs & Technical Notes — Practical writeups covering hardware bring-up, latency-sensitive controller design, and low-maintenance self-hosted infrastructure.

Reach out via our contact page if you think Broken Gadgets R&D can help with your project, or explore our open-source repositories on GitHub.
